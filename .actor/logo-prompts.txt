================================================================================
PRO TIPS for Gemini Nano Banana / Gemini 3 Pro Image
================================================================================

1. Paste each JSON in a CLEAN message — no extra text around it. Nano Banana
   parses JSON well but mixing it with natural-language prose confuses it.

2. To get variations of one design, append a key after generation:
       "seed_variation": 2     (then 3, 4, 5)
   Pick the strongest from 4-6 seeds.

3. Hex codes (#D4AF37) beat named colors (gold) for precision.

4. Iterate conversationally AFTER the first generation:
       "Make the gold more saturated, the navy slightly lighter,
        bug silhouette larger by 15%"
   Nano Banana 3 Pro handles refinement well.

5. Generate at 1024x1024 then downsize to 256x256 for Apify upload.
   Don't ask Nano Banana for tiny output directly — quality suffers.

6. Apify shows logos in a 96-128px circular crop on the store.
   Keep subject in the CENTER 80% safely.

7. Nano Banana doesn't support transparent backgrounds — always specify
   a solid background color in the JSON (we have).
